Track your Hearthstone games on the same phone you play on – no PC, no account.

• Overlay in the empty margin next to the board: what's left in your deck, draw chances, your opponent's played cards and the likely opponent deck
• Automatic recognition of game start, both classes, your deck, drawn cards, turns and the result – on the device, nothing is uploaded
• Match history with turn timeline, win rates per deck and matchup, streaks
• Your collection: sync it from Hearthstone's collection screen; opened packs, crafted and disenchanted cards are followed automatically
• Missing cards and Arcane Dust cost for every deck, cheapest meta decks for your collection, the most useful crafts
• Meta decks from HSReplay.net, deck builder, deck codes, card database
• English and German, follows your Hearthstone language; new sets arrive without an app update

Free and open source (GPL-3.0): https://github.com/stroexd/hs-deck-tracker-android

Feedback and bug reports: https://github.com/stroexd/hs-deck-tracker-android/issues
